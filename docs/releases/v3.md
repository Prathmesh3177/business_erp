## 2. Release Scope & Module Architecture

### 2.1 Core System Capabilities (P00 – P16)

1. **Security & Identity (P02)**:
   - Salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (100k rounds) password hashing.
   - Capability-based Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
   - Append-only redacted audit log (`AuditRedactor`) and idle lock screen modal.
2. **Catalog & Party Masters (P03)**:
   - Configurable attributes for solar panels, inverters, pumps, batteries, and cables.
   - Repeat-safe CSV master import and cost price masking for Counter staff.
3. **Financial Accounting Engine (P04)**:
   - 64-bit paise integer `Money` arithmetic with fixed-point `UnitPrice`, `Quantity`, and `TaxRate`.
   - Balanced double-entry journal invariant ($Dr = Cr$) and Indian GST engine (Intra-state vs Inter-state).
4. **Perpetual Inventory & Serials (P05)**:
   - Append-only stock movements and perpetual weighted-average unit costing per location.
   - Whole-unit serial lifecycle (`inStock`, `reserved`, `sold`, `quarantined`, `scrapped`).
5. **Purchases & Landed Costing (P06)**:
   - Atomic `UnitOfWork` purchase posting with landed cost allocation (by value & quantity) and duplicate invoice protection.
6. **Counter POS & Sales (P07)**:
   - Barcode item search, split tender allocation, draft hold/resume, and credit limit enforcement.
7. **Finance, Returns & Expenses (P08)**:
   - Cumulative return limits, original cost snapshot reversal, purchase debit notes, expense vouchers, and cash register counter session reconciliation.
8. **Invoices, Printing & Barcodes (P09)**:
   - Frozen `InvoiceViewModel`, versioned A4 PDF renderer, 58/80mm thermal renderers, audited reprint copy watermarks, and barcode wedge listener.
9. **Reports & BI (P10)**:
   - Sales summary, GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B tax returns, stock valuation, party ageing, trial balance, and deterministic reorder point alerts.
10. **Portable Backup & Recovery (P11)**:
    - AES-256-GCM encrypted ZIP envelopes (`.erpa`), pre-restore safety snapshots (`.pre_restore_safety.bak`), and post-restore sequence reconciliation.
11. **Mobile Companion Workflows (P12)**:
    - Persistent "Isolated Mobile Data Mode" banner, `MobileCameraAdapter` 1920x1080 JPEG compression with SHA-256 hashing, `AppLifecycleDraftHandler` draft recovery, and multi-transport print detection (Bluetooth/Network/USB OTG).
12. **Solar Projects & Site Costing (P15)**:
    - Solar quotation revision lifecycle, site creation on acceptance without double-posting, material issuance/return to site WIP asset account `1400 Work In Progress`, and final project invoicing with WIP to COGS transfer.
13. **Service, Warranty & AMC (P16)**:
    - Service job tickets with warranty & AMC coverage evaluation, technician assignment, visit recording with spares stock deduction, serial component replacement lineage tracking, AMC contract creation & renewal, and visit limit / expiry reminders.
